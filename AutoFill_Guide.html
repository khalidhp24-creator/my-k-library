How it Works

  1. Validation: The engine cleans your input (ISBN or Title).
  2. Mult-Tier Search: Queries Google, then Open Library, then our 4 marketplace scrapers.
  3. Data Fusion: Intelligent logic merges the best data into a single record.
  4. Local Imaging: Covers are downloaded locally to ensure 100% offline access.
  5. User Review: You approve the final data in the V24 Scrolling Review window.
